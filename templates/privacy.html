Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-29

What we collect

  • Waitlist email (landing page form) — stored to notify you when the public beta launches. Not shared with third parties.
  • Language preference — a small cookie (lang) remembers whether you chose English or Korean. No tracking or advertising purpose.
  • Ratings, reviews, and usage counts you enter on the archive (dashboard) — stored to power search, trend scoring, and recommendations within Runcast itself.

What we don't do (yet)

Runcast currently runs no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. If that changes — for example, adding ad-supported monetization — this page and a cookie-consent notice will be updated before any such script goes live.

Third-party data

Runcast reads public repository metadata from the GitHub API to build the archive. This does not involve your personal data as a visitor.

Data retention & deletion

Waitlist emails are kept until the public beta launches or you ask us to remove them. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

Disclaimer

Runcast archives and helps run skills, commands, plugins, and rules published by third parties on GitHub and elsewhere. Runcast does not write, review, vet, or endorse any archived asset, and cannot guarantee its safety, correctness, or behavior. Any issue, damage, or problem arising from installing or running an archived asset is the responsibility of that asset's original author/publisher — not Runcast. Check an asset's license and source before running it, especially when no license is listed.

아직 하지 않는 것

Runcast는 현재 분석 도구, 광고, 제3자 추적 스크립트를 전혀 사용하지 않습니다. 추후 광고 기반 수익화 등을 추가할 경우, 해당 기능이 실제로 켜지기 전에 이 페이지와 쿠키 동의 배너를 먼저 업데이트합니다.

제3자 데이터

Runcast는 아카이브 구축을 위해 GitHub API에서 공개 저장소 메타데이터를 읽어옵니다. 방문자 개인정보와는 무관합니다.

보관 및 삭제

대기자 명단 이메일은 공개 베타 오픈 시까지, 또는 삭제 요청 시까지 보관합니다. 삭제를 원하시면 아래 연락처로 문의해 주세요.
